# Q4 OddHobb Plan — creative commerce first (2026-10-10)

> Rule until December: new code must improve conversion, make fulfilment
> more reliable, reduce production cost, or launch a product customers
> can buy immediately.

## Priorities

| Priority | Focus | Success |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | Personalised cards | Upload → preview → checkout → fulfilment works |
| P0 | Existing personalised gifts | Figurines, ornaments, keychains purchasable |
| P0 | Etsy conversion | Complete listings: images, delivery times, personalisation |
| P0 | Pinterest distribution | Pins + ads to purchase-ready products |
| P1 | Creative expansion | New designs from clicks/saves/purchases |
| P2 | Gift collections | Bundles after individual demand proven |
| P3 | Custom hardware/agents | After manufacturing + cash flow support it |

## 14-day sprint

1. **Catalogue excellent** — 5–8 strong listings, not 50 mediocre. Cards, pet figures, ornaments, best gifts. Every listing: real imagery, hero, dims, materials, personalisation instructions, honest delivery, priced with real costs.
2. **Buying journey working** — Etsy/Pinterest → configure → pay → photos → correct design. Shopify: payment, storage, address, fulfilment verified.
3. **Distribution experiment** — Pinterest organic + capped Etsy Ads on strongest listings. One paid channel, not three.
4. **New products from demand** — weekly review of searches/clicks/saves/buys → variations from existing assets.

## The metric

```
Profit = Revenue − production − shipping − fees − ads − refund allowance
```

Cards = entry + organic. Figurines/gift orders = paid ads (margins support it).

## What happens to the rest

- **Funnier:** alive, future organic channel for comedy cards. No credits economy needed to sell cards.
- **Pogtown:** small experiment, excellent shareable characters.
- **Compiler:** one real supplier integration at a time, when a sold product needs it.
- **Shenzhen vision:** easier to finance once the cycle works.

## The cycle

Find gift opportunity → beautiful design → publish listing → acquire customer → fulfil reliably → use results to design better.
